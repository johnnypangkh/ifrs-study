## 1. Frozen story (2–5 sentences) — ch03 only

The learner builds **initial cost** of PPE: after recognition, the item is measured at **cost** (IAS 16.15). Cost **comprises** three limbs (IAS 16.16) — **purchase price** · **directly attributable costs** bringing the asset to location and condition capable of operating as intended · **initial estimate of dismantling / removal / restoration** (IAS 37 obligation when incurred on acquisition or from use other than producing inventories). Attachments stay lighter: **exclusions** (IAS 16.19) as muted “not in cost”; **cease capitalising** when capable of operating as intended (IAS 16.20) as a constraint on the DA limb; **proceeds before intended use (20A)** → P&L not cost reduction (Map tip only — full Illus **3.5.1** stays body); self-constructed / bearer (22 / 22A) nest under Cost (same principles); cash-price equivalent / deferred (23) and exchanges (24–26) as secondary measurement facets; IAS 23 borrowing · IAS 20 grants · IFRIC 1 changes = dashed tips. **What this is not:** an IAS 23 borrowing-costs course, an IFRIC 1 remeasurement course, a purchase flowchart, a 20A worked-strip dump on the Map spine, or a subsequent-models / depreciation chapter.

## 2. Learner visit path (eye walk · L→R then T→B · 4–8 steps)

Geometry must force: enter **Cost** formula board (result distinct, continuing route **column 1**) → three limbs L→R under unboxed operators (collection inside Cost) → nest limb detail inside each term → muted “not in cost” nest + stop-capitalising constraint on DA → lighter secondaries (20A tip · self-constructed · cash-price · exchanges) → dashed tips last. Dead-ends do not feed Cost. No rival Decision hub on this board.

1. **Enter** at the **Cost identity** (top-left / top spine): PPE measured at **cost** at recognition. Result card **Cost** is visually distinct (Kit 4). Chip `3.0`. Continuing exit later → Subsequent models (`ch04`).  
2. **Cost-sum row (Kit 4):** **Cost = Purchase price + Directly attributable costs + Initial dismantling / restoration estimate** — operators unboxed and centred on the midline; three limbs same-format peer terms (**collection** by operators); prefer **result-first** one-row layout (`Cost = A + B + C`) unless Johnny picks another Equations-and-sums layout (open Q1). Chips `3.1`.  
3. **Inside each limb (nested residual / containment):**  
   - **Purchase price** — duties, non-refundable taxes, net of discounts / rebates. Chip `3.1`.  
   - **Directly attributable (DA)** — bring to location / condition capable of operating as intended (examples nest: employee benefits · site preparation · delivery · installation · testing costs under 17(e)). Chip `3.2`.  
   - **Dismantling / restoration** — initial estimate of IAS 37 obligation; **dashed IAS 37**; muted tip IAS 16.18 inventory-production carve (dismantling for inventories → IAS 2, not PPE cost). Chip `3.4`.  
4. **Muted nest beside / under Cost (not a rival spine):** **Costs that are not included** (opening new facility · introducing new product / service · conducting business in new location · admin / general overhead) → muted leaf **Not in cost / expense**. Chip `3.3`.  
5. **Constraint on the DA limb (link, not a second Decision hub):** **Cease capitalising** when the asset is **capable of operating as intended by management** — spanning bar or labelled constraint across / on DA (open Q2). Idle / under-utilised after that point does not keep adding to cost. Chip `3.2` (para 20 home with DA).  
6. **Lighter secondary (beside what it qualifies — not second hub):** **Proceeds before intended use (20A)** — proceeds from selling items produced while bringing to location/condition → **P&L**; cost of those items → **P&L** (IAS 2 measurement); **do not** reduce PPE cost. Map = tip chip only; **Illus 3.5.1 mandatory body**. Incidental operations (para 21) = thinner tip beside 20A. Chip `3.5`.  
7. **Still lighter under / beside Cost plate:**  
   - **Self-constructed** — same principles as acquired; eliminate internal profits; exclude abnormal waste; bearer plants tip (22A). Chip `3.6`.  
   - **Cash-price equivalent / deferred payment** — cost = cash-price equivalent; difference = interest over credit period. Chip `3.7`.  
   - **Exchanges** — measure at FV unless lack commercial substance or FV not reliably measurable → then CA of asset given up. Chip `3.8`.  
8. **Dashed tips last (near what they qualify) + body-only:** IAS 23 borrowing (qualifying asset may enter cost) beside Cost / elements · IAS 20 grants tip · IFRIC 1 changes in existing decommissioning (Models-adjacent — initial estimate only here) · checklist `3.11` · all Illus strips body.

Narration self-test (no “look around for…”):  
`3.0 Cost → 3.1 Cost = purchase + DA + dismantling → nest limb detail → [muted 3.3 not in cost] · [constraint 3.2 cease when capable] → tips 3.5 20A · 3.6 self-construct · 3.7 cash-price · 3.8 exchanges → dashed IAS 23 / IAS 20 / IFRIC 1 → chip to ch04`.
