## 1. Frozen story (2–5 sentences) — ch01 only

The learner decides whether a tangible item is **PPE in the scope of IAS 16**, or another Standard prevails / a carve-out applies. Membership requires **all** of the PPE definition limbs (tangible · held for use in production/supply/rental/admin · expected use during more than one period). When IAS 16.3 excludes the item (held for sale · biological assets other than bearer plants · exploration and evaluation · mineral rights/reserves), the continuing PPE route stops — except **PPE used to develop or maintain** those assets, which stays in IAS 16. Cost-model investment property under IAS 40 is a **measurement bridge** into IAS 16’s cost model (not a second hub); bearer plants are in scope (produce out); lessee ROU and spare-parts mechanics are tips only. **What this is not:** an IAS 40 / IFRS 16 / IAS 41 / IFRS 5 course, a Venn of Standards, or a dump of boundary Illus strips onto the Map spine.

## 2. Learner visit path (eye walk · L→R then T→B · 4–8 steps)

Geometry must force: enter definition Decision → Yes stem **into** the “in IAS 16” plate (continuing route **column 1**) → peers under spanning bar inside the plate → exclusion Decision with muted/stop exits **column 3** → carve-back recovery into the Yes plate → lighter tips last. Dead-ends do not feed the main zone.

1. **Enter** at the **PPE definition Decision** (top-left): Does this item meet the PPE definition (all limbs)? Chip `1.1`.  
2. **No arm** → muted leaf **Not PPE** (column 3) — may be inventory, intangible, financial asset, etc.; no continuing stem into the main plate. Body Illus and FAQs stay in body.  
3. **Yes arm** → labelled **flow** stem **enters** the **“In IAS 16”** containment plate (continuing route stays column 1).  
4. **Inside the Yes plate — Kit 3:** three same-format peer cards — **Tangible** · **Held for use** (production / supply of goods or services, rental to others, or administration) · **More than one period** — under one spanning **“all of these”** bar (link, no arrowheads between peers). Chips `1.1`.  
5. **Still inside / next Decision:** Excluded under IAS 16.3? **Yes (excluded)** → one **red** leaf **IAS 16 does not apply** (column 3) with four **muted** carve-out exits beside it and dashed see-also nested in the leaf: IFRS 5 HFS · IAS 41 (non-bearer biological) · IFRS 6 E&E · mineral rights/reserves. Chip `1.3`. **Develop/maintain carve-back** = labelled recovery **flow** from that leaf back into the Yes plate (PPE used to develop or maintain those assets stays in IAS 16) — do not invent a fifth exclusion.  
6. **No (not excluded)** → continuing outcome inside the plate: **In scope of IAS 16** (column 1) — chip forward toward Recognition (`ch02`). Nest **bearer plants** as an in-scope note inside the plate (produce on bearer plants out — dashed IAS 41); thin tip to Initial measurement for self-constructed treatment (22A). Chip `1.5`.  
7. **Lighter secondary (below / beside what they qualify — not equal spine):** cost-model investment property **bridge** (IAS 16.5) as neutral peer annotation + dashed IAS 40 (`1.4`); lessee ROU dashed tip IFRS 16 — depreciation uses IAS 16 (`1.7`); spare parts **pointer only** → Recognition (`1.8`).  
8. **Body only (Map chips, no worked strips on spine):** Objective (`1.0`); boundary Illus hotel · base stock · research · gold (`1.6`); mineral-rights Illus (`1.3.1`); Scope checklist (`1.9`).

Narration self-test (no “look around for…”):  
`1.1 definition? → [No: Not PPE] | [Yes → in-IAS-16 plate] → peers under “all of these” → 1.3 excluded? → [red stop + muted carve-outs / carve-back in] | [in scope] → tips 1.4 · 1.5 · 1.7 · 1.8 → chip to ch02`.
