## 1. Frozen story (2–5 sentences) — ch05 only

From either subsequent model (ch04 bridge), the entity **systematically allocates the depreciable amount over useful life** (IAS 16.50). Depreciable amount = amount substituted for cost (or revalued amount) **minus residual value**; residual is current end-of-life disposal estimate (IAS 16.6, 53). **Significant parts** with different useful lives or methods are depreciated separately — **containment** inside the asset (IAS 16.43–47), not a third model. Methods that reflect the pattern of consumption sit as **peers** (straight-line ∥ diminishing balance ∥ units-of-production) under a spanning rule; a **revenue-based method is not appropriate** (IAS 16.62A) = muted / missing route, not an equal peer. Depreciation **begins** when the asset is available for use and **ceases** at the earlier of IFRS 5 held-for-sale classification or derecognition — **idle ≠ stop** (IAS 16.55). Review residual, useful life, and method at least annually (prospective change in estimate). Charge usually hits P&L, or may be absorbed into another asset’s carrying amount (IAS 16.48–49). **What this is not:** a models re-dump (ch04), an IAS 36 impairment course, an IFRS 16 ROU measurement course, a replacements/inspections FAQ dump (ch02 home), a method-worked-strip dump on the spine, or a third “choose dep instead of a model” peer.
