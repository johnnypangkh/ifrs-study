## 1. Frozen story (2–5 sentences) — ch06 only

The entity **removes the carrying amount** of an item of PPE when it is **disposed of** or when **no future economic benefits** are expected from its use or disposal (IAS 16.67). On that exit, **gain or loss** = net disposal proceeds − carrying amount and is included in **profit or loss** — gains are **not** classified as revenue (IAS 16.68 · 71); consideration for the disposal is determined under **IFRS 15** transfer guidance (IAS 16.72 tip). Side events stay off the equal spine: **compensation** receivable is accounted for as **separate events** from impairment, derecognition, and replacement cost (IAS 16.65–66); **replacement** capitalises the new part and **derecognises** the replaced part’s CA (IAS 16.13 / 70 — bridge from ch02); assets held for rental then sold take the **68A** path (inventories → IFRS 15 revenue); **sale-and-leaseback** is a thin **IFRS 16** tip; **IFRS 5** held-for-sale exits IAS 16 measurement (dashed). **What this is not:** a purchase/sale flowchart, a compensation FAQ dump on the spine, an IFRS 15 revenue course, an IFRS 16 lease course, an IFRS 5 measurement course, a partial-disposal / undivided-interest densify (gap), or a disclosure package (ch07).
