## 1. Frozen story (2–5 sentences) — ch04 only

After recognition the entity chooses, **by entire class**, either the **cost model** or the **revaluation model** (IAS 16.29–31, 36). Both peers remain on the continuing spine (neither is a dead end); **both models still depreciate** — systematic allocation lives in ch05 (chip / bridge only; do **not** draw depreciation as a third model peer). Cost model: carry at cost less accumulated depreciation less accumulated impairment. Revaluation model: carry at revalued amount (FV at revaluation date less subsequent dep / impairment) with sufficient regularity; nest lighter revaluation mechanics — eliminate vs gross (para 35), increases / decreases (OCI/surplus vs P&L, para 39–40), surplus transfers (para 41, not through P&L). Class / simultaneity / rolling nest under the spanning class-policy bar. IAS 36 pointer dashed only (para 63); IFRIC 1 tip lighter; 29A–29B thin; compensation tip → ch06. **What this is not:** a depreciation course, an IAS 36 impairment course, an IFRS 13 fair-value course, an IFRIC 1 remeasurement course, a revaluation-FAQ dump on the spine, or a third “depreciation model” peer.

## 2. Learner visit path (eye walk · L→R then T→B · 4–8 steps)

Geometry must force: enter class-policy / after-recognition beat → two same-format model peers under spanning bar (continuing route **column 1** for both) → nest class / simultaneity / rolling under the bar → lighter nests inside each peer → both-depreciate chip → dashed tips last. Neither peer is a dead end. No rival Decision hub inventing “cost vs reval vs dep”. No arrowheads between equal-rank model peers.

1. **Enter** at **After recognition — choose a model** (top-left / top spine): accounting policy for an **entire class** of PPE — cost model **or** revaluation model (IAS 16.29). Chip `4.0`. Continuing exit later → Depreciation (`ch05`) from **both** peers.  
2. **Kit 3 peer row:** **Cost model ∥ Revaluation model** — same-format peer cards L→R under one spanning **“policy by entire class”** bar (**link**, no arrowheads between peers). Both stay on the continuing spine. Chips `4.2` · `4.3` (peer cards) with class rule at `4.1`.  
3. **Under / inside the spanning bar area (nest, not equal peers):** **Class** = grouping of assets of similar nature and use; if one item revalued → **entire class** revalued; **simultaneous** revaluation (avoid mixed cost/value dates); **rolling** only if class completed within a short period and kept up to date. Thin tips: Official class examples · geography alone ≠ separate class · AUC policy option · FV not reliably measurable → those assets under cost (muted annotation inside Revaluation). Chip `4.1`. Illus **4.1.1–4.1.3** body only.  
4. **Inside Cost peer (lighter carrying-amount identity nest):** Carry at **cost − accumulated depreciation − accumulated impairment losses**. Cost base unchanged for market movements; impairment still via IAS 36 (dashed). Chip `4.2`.  
5. **Inside Revaluation peer (lighter nests — not equal third peers to Cost):**  
   - **Revalued amount** = FV at revaluation date − subsequent accum dep − subsequent accum impairment; **sufficient regularity** so CA does not differ materially from FV at period-end. **Dashed IFRS 13**. Chip `4.3`.  
   - **Mechanical methods** (para 35): eliminate accum dep against gross **or** restate gross proportionately — same net CA / same gain. Chip `4.4`. Illus **4.4.1–4.4.2** body.  
   - **Increases / decreases** (para 39–40): increase → OCI (revaluation surplus) except reverse prior P&L decrease; decrease → P&L except to extent of surplus. Chip `4.5`. Illus **4.5.1–4.5.4** body.  
   - **Surplus transfer** (para 41): may transfer to RE as used / on derecognition — **not through P&L**. Chip `4.6`. Illus **4.6.1** body; thin tip → ch06.  
6. **Shared continuing bridge (both peers):** **Both models still depreciate** — chip / labelled bridge → Depreciation (`ch05`). Do **not** add Depreciation as a third peer under the class bar.  
7. **Lighter secondaries / tips (beside what they qualify):** Optional **29A–29B** thin note (`4.7`); **IAS 8.17** adopt-revaluation tip (current-period revaluation, not prior-period restatement); **IAS 40** cost-model lock-in tip (para 5); voluntary FV disclosure tip on Cost peer (para 79(d)).  
8. **Dashed tips last + body-only:** **IAS 36** pointer (para 63) · **IFRIC 1** under cost / reval rules (`4.9`; Illus **4.9.1** body) · **IAS 12** tip on surplus (`4.6` / para 42) · **compensation** tip → ch06 (`4.10`) · checklist `4.11` · all Illus strips body.

Narration self-test (no “look around for…”):  
`4.0 after recognition — policy by entire class → Cost ∥ Revaluation peers under spanning bar → nest 4.1 class/simultaneity/rolling → Cost identity nest 4.2 | Reval nests 4.3 regularity · 4.4 mechanical · 4.5 inc/dec · 4.6 surplus transfer → both depreciate → chip ch05 → dashed IAS 36 / IFRS 13 / IFRIC 1 / IAS 12 / compensation→ch06`.
