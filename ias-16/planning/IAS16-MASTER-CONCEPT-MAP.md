## 1. Frozen story (2–5 sentences) — pack master

The entity decides whether a tangible item held for use over more than one period is **PPE in the scope of IAS 16** (definition and carve-outs). When the recognition criteria are met — probable future economic benefits and reliably measurable cost — the item is **recognised as an asset and measured initially at cost**, whose elements (purchase price, directly attributable costs, and qualifying dismantling/restoration obligations) sit **inside** that cost amount. After recognition the entity chooses, **by entire class**, either the **cost model** or the **revaluation model**; under either model it **systematically allocates the depreciable amount over useful life**, usually **part by significant part**. The carrying amount leaves when the item is disposed of or no future benefits are expected; **disclosure** packages the class roll-forward and policy story as a **secondary** band (not an equal life-arc peer). **What this is not:** an IAS 36 impairment course, an IAS 40 investment-property course, an IFRS 16 lease-accounting course, a dump of every nested chapter card onto the pack spine, or an invented “buy → install → operate → sell” process flowchart — those Standards appear only as **see-also** tips beside the PPE phase they qualify; chapter Maps remain the zoom boards.
