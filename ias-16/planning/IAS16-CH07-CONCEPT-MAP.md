## 1. Frozen story (2–5 sentences) — ch07 only

IAS 16 Disclosure assembles the **PPE disclosure package by class** (IAS 16.73–79 · 74A). The core **mandatory** package discloses, for each class: accounting-policy bases / methods / lives (73(a)–(c), with 75/76 companions); gross carrying amount and accumulated depreciation (aggregated with impairment) open and close (73(d)); a **reconciliation of carrying amount** with the nine 73(e) limbs; and additional 74 disclosures (restrictions, CIP expenditures, contractual commitments). **74A** adds proceeds-before-intended-use amounts and compensation received (bridge ch03 20A + ch06 compensation); **revalued-asset extras** (para 77) sit beside IFRS 13; **encouraged** useful information (para 79) is lighter rank, not mandatory. Related Standards (IAS 1, IFRS 13, IAS 23, IFRS 5, IFRS 16 ROU, IAS 36 via 78) are dashed tips only. **What this is not:** a life-arc Decision fork (ch01–ch06); a full FS notes dump / checklist as equal spine peers; an IFRS 13 fair-value course; an IAS 23 borrowing-cost course; an IFRS 5/16 course; densify of every FAQ; Illus worked strips on the Map; inventing CU/entity plates; or the pack master Map.
