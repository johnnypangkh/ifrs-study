7.0 Disclosure objective — by classtop

The financial statements shall disclose, for each class of property, plant and equipment, the measurement bases, depreciation methods, useful lives or rates, gross carrying amount and accumulated depreciation (aggregated with accumulated impairment losses) at the beginning and end of the period, and a reconciliation of the carrying amount.   • Class is the disclosure continuum. Para 73 applies for each class. Class definition and the simultaneous-revaluation / rolling-basis rules live in Chapter 4 — the disclosure note follows the same class grain used for the measurement-model policy. Significant parts are depreciated separately (Chapter 5) but are not separately disclosed as classes — for example an airline discloses aircraft as a class, not each engine / airframe part. 7.1 Accounting-policy disclosures for PPEtop

For each class, disclose (a) the measurement bases used for determining the gross carrying amount; (b) the depreciation methods used; and (c) the useful lives or the depreciation rates used. IAS 16 · para 73(a)–(c) PwC MOA 2020 Ch22 · 22.111 DTT iGAAP 2022 A7 · 11.2.1 EY iGAAP 2026 Ch19 · section 8.1

  • Measurement bases (73(a)). State cost or revaluation (or both across different classes). When more than one basis appears in the entity, the gross carrying amount for that basis in each category may need to be shown — and if the revaluation model is adopted for a class, the entire class must be revalued. IAS 16 · para 73(a) IAS 16 · para 31 EY iGAAP 2026 Ch19 · section 8.1
  • Depreciation methods and lives / rates (73(b)–(c)). Selection of method and estimation of useful life are matters of judgement. Disclosure lets users review the policies and compare entities. IAS 16 · para 73(b)–(c) IAS 16 · para 75 EY iGAAP 2026 Ch19 · section 8.1 DTT iGAAP 2022 A7 · 11.2.1
  • Para 75 companion amounts. For similar reasons, disclose depreciation recognised in profit or loss or as part of the cost of other assets during the period, and accumulated depreciation at the end of the period. IAS 16 · para 75 EY iGAAP 2026 Ch19 · section 8.1
  • IAS 8 estimate-change disclosures (para 76). Disclose the nature and effect of a change in accounting estimate that affects the current or subsequent periods — for PPE this commonly arises from changes in residual values, estimated dismantling / restoration costs, useful lives, or depreciation methods. 7.2 Gross carrying amount and accumulated depreciationtop

Disclose the gross carrying amount and the accumulated depreciation (aggregated with accumulated impairment losses) at the beginning and end of the period — for each class. IAS 16 · para 73(d) DTT iGAAP 2022 A7 · 11.2.1 EY iGAAP 2026 Ch19 · section 8.1 PwC MOA 2020 Ch22 · 22.111

  • Accumulated depreciation is aggregated with accumulated impairment losses in the open/close stock figures — do not present impairment separately from accumulated depreciation in this 73(d) pair (impairment movements still appear in the reconciliation under 73(e)(iv)–(vi)). IAS 16 · para 73(d) IAS 16 · para 73(e)
  • Cost or revalued amount. Under the cost model the gross column is cost; under the revaluation model it is the revalued gross (or the eliminated-net presentation, depending on the mechanical method in Chapter 4) — PwC describes the movement table as explaining movements in cost, or revalued amount, and depreciation for each class. PwC MOA 2020 Ch22 · 22.111 IAS 16 · para 73(d)

Beginning of period

  • Gross carrying amount (cost or revalued gross)
  • Accumulated depreciation aggregated with accumulated impairment losses
  • Implied carrying amount = gross − accum

End of period

  • Same two stock figures for the class
  • Bridge between open and close = reconciliation in 7.3
  • Para 75 also wants period depreciation and end-of-period accumulated depreciation

7.3 Reconciliation of carrying amounttop

Disclose a reconciliation of the carrying amount at the beginning and end of the period showing the prescribed movement lines. IAS 16 · para 73(e) KPMG Insights 2019/20 · 3.2 · 3.2.400.10 DTT iGAAP 2022 A7 · 11.2.1 EY iGAAP 2026 Ch19 · section 8.1 PwC MOA 2020 Ch22 · 22.111

  • Comparative period too (KPMG). The reconciliation covers the current reporting period and also the beginning and end of the comparative reporting period. KPMG Insights 2019/20 · 3.2 · 3.2.400.10 IAS 16 · para 73(e)
  • Additions and business combinations are separate lines. Acquisitions are split between PPE acquired in a business combination and other acquisitions (additions). Disposals, however, are presented in a single line item (held-for-sale / disposal-group exits under IFRS 5 together with other disposals). Split acquisitions (KPMG)

  • Line (i) additions = other acquisitions (ordinary purchases / self-constructed capitalisations).
  • Line (iii) business combinations = PPE acquired through a business combination.
  • Do not collapse the two into one “acquisitions” total — the reconciliation keeps them separate.

Combine disposals (KPMG)

  • Line (ii) covers IFRS 5 held-for-sale / disposal-group exits and other disposals in a single line.
  • Unlike acquisitions, exits are not split between “IFRS 5” and “other” in the prescribed limbs.
  • Apply the pattern inside each PPE class column; repeat for the comparative period.

7.4 Additional disclosures — restrictions, construction, commitmentstop

The financial statements shall also disclose (a) the existence and amounts of restrictions on title, and PPE pledged as security for liabilities; (b) the amount of expenditures recognised in the carrying amount of an item of PPE in the course of its construction; and (c) the amount of contractual commitments for the acquisition of PPE. IAS 16 · para 74 DTT iGAAP 2022 A7 · 11.2.1 EY iGAAP 2026 Ch19 · section 8.1 PwC MOA 2020 Ch22 · 22.111

  • Restrictions / pledges (74(a)). Title restrictions and amounts of PPE pledged as security for liabilities. IAS 16 · para 74(a)
  • Construction expenditures (74(b)) — DTT drafting note. Although the Standard’s drafting is a little unclear, DTT reads 74(b) as requiring disclosure of the cost of assets that are still in the course of construction at the reporting date (rather than assets that were under construction when costs were incurred). On whether the amount is costs incurred in the year or cumulative costs on such assets, DTT favours the cumulative reading as more logical. DTT iGAAP 2022 A7 · 11.2.1 IAS 16 · para 74(b)
  • Contractual commitments (74(c)). Amount of contractual commitments for the acquisition of PPE. IAS 16 · para 74(c)
  • Compensation in P&L moved to 74A(a). Pre-May-2020 drafting housed third-party compensation disclosure in 74(d); the amendments moved it to 74A(a) — see 7.5. IAS 16 · para 74A(a) DTT iGAAP 2022 A7 · 11.2.1

74(a) Restrictions / security

  • Existence and amounts
  • Title restrictions
  • PPE pledged as security for liabilities

74(b) Under construction

  • Expenditures in carrying amount while under construction
  • Still under construction at period-end (DTT view): prefer cumulative cost on those assets

74(c) Commitments

  • Contractual commitments to acquire PPE
  • Forward-looking cash / obligation signal

7.5 Proceeds before intended use and compensation amounts (74A)top

If not presented separately in the statement of comprehensive income, the financial statements shall also disclose (a) the amount of compensation from third parties for items of PPE that were impaired, lost or given up that is included in profit or loss; and (b) the amounts of proceeds and cost included in profit or loss in accordance with paragraph 20A that relate to items produced that are not an output of the entity’s ordinary activities, and which line item(s) include such proceeds and cost. IAS 16 · para 74A DTT iGAAP 2022 A7 · 11.2.1 EY iGAAP 2026 Ch19 · section 8.1

  • 74A(a) — compensation included in P&L. Disclose the amount when it is not separately presented on the face. Recognition timing and the four separate events live in Chapter 6 (para 65–66). IAS 16 · para 74A(a) IAS 16 · para 65–66
  • 74A(b) — 20A proceeds and cost. For items produced while bringing PPE to intended location/condition that are not an output of ordinary activities: disclose proceeds amount, cost amount, and the SOC line item(s) that include them — unless already separately presented. Measurement path → Chapter 3. IAS 16 · para 74A(b) IAS 16 · para 20A DTT iGAAP 2022 A7 · 11.2.1
  • May 2020 amendment package. The Board moved former 74(d) compensation disclosure into 74A(a) and added 74A(b) alongside the 20A P&L path. Effective for annual periods beginning on or after 1 January 2022. DTT iGAAP 2022 A7 · 11.2.1 IAS 16 · para 74A IAS 16 · para 80D

74A(a) Compensation in P&L

  • Disclose the amount of third-party compensation for PPE that was impaired, lost or given up included in profit or loss.
  • Trigger only if that amount is not separately presented in the statement of comprehensive income.
  • Recognition / four separate events → Ch6 · 6.6 (para 65–66).

74A(b) 20A proceeds and cost

  • Disclose proceeds and cost included in P&L under para 20A for items produced that are not ordinary-activity output.
  • Also disclose which SOC line item(s) include those proceeds and cost.
  • Skip the note limb when the amounts are already separately presented on the face. Measurement → Ch3 · 3.5.

7.6 Revalued-asset extrastop

If items of PPE are stated at revalued amounts, disclose — in addition to the disclosures required by IFRS 13 — (a) the effective date of the revaluation; (b) whether an independent valuer was involved; (e) for each revalued class, the carrying amount that would have been recognised had the assets been carried under the cost model; and (f) the revaluation surplus, indicating the change for the period and any restrictions on distribution of the balance to shareholders. Illustration: 7.6.1 — Cost-model comparative includes capitalised borrowing costs DTT iGAAP 2022 A7 · 11.2.2-1 KPMG Insights 2019/20 · 3.2 · 3.2.400.40 EY iGAAP 2026 Ch19 · section 8.2 PwC MOA 2020 Ch22 · 22.113 IAS 16 · para 77(e) IAS 23 · para 4(a)

Facts

  • Entity K owns a self-constructed manufacturing plant measured at fair value under the IAS 16 revaluation model.
  • As permitted by IAS 23 · para 4(a) for assets measured under the revaluation model, Entity K did not capitalise borrowing costs during construction.
  • Para 77(e) requires disclosure, for each revalued class, of the carrying amount that would have been recognised had the assets been carried under the cost model.

Assessment

  • If Entity K had carried the plant at cost during construction, IAS 23 would have required capitalisation of borrowing costs.
  • The amount disclosed under 77(e) must therefore include the effect of those borrowing costs — gross amount that would have been capitalised less subsequent depreciation.
  • IFRS Interpretations Committee (May 2014 IFRIC Update / IU 05-14) confirmed this reading; PwC / EY / KPMG / DTT all teach the same inclusion.

Conclusion

  • 77(e) is a hypothetical cost-model figure, not a dump of the revaluation-model ledger.
  • IAS 23’s revaluation-model exemption from capitalisation does not remove borrowing costs from the 77(e) comparative.
  • Asset-register detail must be rich enough to rebuild that cost-model carrying amount.

7.7 Optional useful informationtop

Users of financial statements may also find the following information relevant. Therefore, entities are encouraged to disclose these amounts — they are not mandatory. IAS 16 · para 79 PwC MOA 2020 Ch22 · 22.115 DTT iGAAP 2022 A7 · 11.2.3 EY iGAAP 2026 Ch19 · section 8.3

  • 79(a) — carrying amount of temporarily idle PPE.
  • 79(b) — gross carrying amount of any fully depreciated PPE that is still in use.
  • 79(c) — carrying amount of PPE retired from active use and not classified as held for sale under IFRS 5.
  • 79(d) — when the cost model is used, the fair value of PPE when this is materially different from the carrying amount. IAS 16 · para 79

Illustration: 7.7.1 — Idle PPE and postponed construction — when significance pushes disclosure DTT iGAAP 2022 A7 · 11.2.3-1 IAS 16 · para 74(b) IAS 16 · para 79(a) IAS 1 · para 112(c)

Facts

  • IFRIC (now the IFRS Interpretations Committee) was asked how far disclosures are required for PPE that is temporarily idle and for assets under construction when further construction has been postponed.
  • The item was not taken onto the IFRIC agenda.
  • IAS 16 · para 74(b) requires disclosure of expenditures recognised in the carrying amount of PPE in the course of construction; para 79(a) encourages disclosure of temporarily idle PPE.

Assessment

  • IAS 1 · para 112(c) requires notes information that is not presented elsewhere but is relevant to an understanding of the financial statements.
  • In combination, IAS 16 and IAS 1 create an expectation that when the amount of idle assets or postponed construction projects becomes significant, those amounts will be separately disclosed.
  • Conclusions recorded in the May 2009 IFRIC Update (DTT 11.2.3-1).

Conclusion

  • Para 79 is “encouraged,” but significance + IAS 1 relevance can effectively require separate disclosure of idle / postponed-CIP amounts.
  • Do not treat 79(a) as forever optional once the balance is material to users.
  • 74(b) CIP disclosure remains mandatory regardless of the idle analysis.
