4.1Accounting policy for an entire class

  • Choose the cost model or the revaluation model as the accounting policy, and apply that policy to an entire class (similar nature and use).

How the class is revalued

Simultaneously

Avoids selective revaluation and amounts that mix costs and values as at different dates.

Rolling basis

Permitted if the class is revalued within a short period and the revaluations are kept up to date.

4.3Revaluation model

4.4Restate the gross carrying amount consistently, or eliminate accumulated depreciation against it.

Restate the gross carrying amount

Adjust the gross carrying amount consistently with the revaluation (by observable market data or proportionately). Accumulated depreciation = gross carrying amount − carrying amount after accumulated impairment losses.

Eliminate accumulated depreciation

Eliminate accumulated depreciation against the gross carrying amount.

The adjustment to accumulated depreciation forms part of the increase or decrease (4.5).
