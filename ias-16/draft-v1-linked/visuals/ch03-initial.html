3.0Cost

Cost

  • Cash or cash equivalents paid, or fair value of other consideration given, at acquisition or construction.
  • Cash price equivalent at the recognition date.

3.2Directly attributable costs

Bringing the asset to the location and condition necessary for it to be capable of operating in the manner intended by management.

  • Employee benefits arising directly from construction or acquisition
  • Site preparation
  • Initial delivery and handling
  • Installation and assembly
  • Testing whether the asset is functioning properly
  • Professional fees

Depreciation of right-of-use assets used to construct, add to, replace part of or service PPE may form part of cost.

See also IFRS 16

3.4Dismantling, removal and restoration

  • Initial estimate of dismantling, removal and site restoration.
  • Obligation incurred on acquisition, or through use in a period other than to produce inventories.
  • Obligation from producing inventories: IAS 2.

See also IAS 2

3.3Not costs of PPE

  • Opening a new facility
  • Introducing a new product or service (including advertising)
  • New location or class of customer (including training)
  • Administration and general overheads

Directly attributable costs: when recognition ceases

Recognition of costs in the carrying amount ceases when the asset is in the location and condition necessary for it to be capable of operating in the manner intended by management.

Not included: costs of using or redeploying the item

  • Costs while capable of operating as intended but not yet brought into use, or operated below full capacity
  • Initial operating losses
  • Costs of relocating or reorganising operations

Measurement of cost

3.6Self-constructed asset

  • Same principles as an acquired asset.
  • Similar assets made for sale: usually the cost of constructing an asset for sale.
  • Internal profits are eliminated. Abnormal waste is excluded.
  • Bearer plants: same as a self-constructed asset until capable of operating as intended.

See also IAS 23

3.7Payment deferred beyond normal credit terms

  • Cost is the cash price equivalent.
  • The difference to total payment is interest over the credit period, unless it is capitalised under IAS 23.

See also IAS 23

3.8Exchange of non-monetary assets

  • Measured at fair value unless the exchange lacks commercial substance, or neither fair value is reliably measurable; then the carrying amount of the asset given up.
  • Commercial substance: the cash-flow configuration differs, or entity-specific value changes, and the difference is significant.
  • Fair value of the asset given up is used unless the asset received is more clearly evident.

3.7AContingent / variable consideration

When the future payment relates to the cost of the asset: two approaches observed.

Cost accumulation

  • Not included on initial recognition.
  • Added to cost when incurred, or when a related liability is remeasured.
∥

Financial-liability model

  • Estimate recorded with a matching liability on initial recognition.
  • Remeasurements and further payments → profit or loss, or capitalised.

Shown side by side and not reconciled. Apply one policy consistently; disclose when material.

Subsequent change: capitalise only if linked to the asset initially received — Illus stay in body.
