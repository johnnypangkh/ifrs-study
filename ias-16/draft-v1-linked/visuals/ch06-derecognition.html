Derecognise on disposal or when no future economic benefits are expected

6.0Derecognition — remove carrying amount

When to derecognise

Disposal

OR

No future economic benefits expected

  • By sale, finance lease or donation.
  • Date of disposal: when the recipient obtains control (IFRS 15).
  • From use or disposal.
  • When permanently withdrawn from use and no future economic benefits are expected, derecognise even without a sale.

Still on books — Idle or mothballed status alone does not trigger derecognition; depreciation continues until held for sale or derecognition (see Depreciation).

Cost model

Profit or loss.

Revaluation model

Profit or loss, as under the cost model.

Usually small when revaluations are kept up to date; a large gain or loss may signal an out-of-date valuation.

Special paths

6.5Replaced part

  • The carrying amount of the replaced part is derecognised, whether or not the replaced part had been depreciated separately.
  • If it is not practicable to determine that carrying amount, the cost of the replacement may be used as an indication of what the cost of the replaced part was at the time it was acquired or constructed.

2.6See also Replacement capitalised under Recognition — derecognise the replaced carrying amount.

6.3Held for rental then sold

  • Transfer such assets to inventories at their carrying amount when they cease to be rented and become held for sale.
  • The proceeds from the sale of such assets are recognised as revenue in accordance with IFRS 15.
  • IFRS 5 does not apply when assets that are held for sale in the ordinary course of business are transferred to inventories.

6.0APartial disposal

The item derecognised need not be the unit first recognised — physical part or undivided interest.

Physical part

  • Allocate carrying amount on a systematic basis; derecognise that part.
  • Gain or loss equals proceeds minus allocated carrying amount.
∥

Undivided interest

  • Proportionate share of the whole asset (ownership in common).
  • Derecognise that fraction of carrying amount when control is ceded.

6.6Compensation for impairment

  • Compensation from third parties for items of property, plant and equipment that were impaired, lost or given up is included in profit or loss when the compensation becomes receivable.
  • Impairments or losses of items of property, plant and equipment, related claims for or payments of compensation from third parties, and any subsequent purchase or construction of replacement assets are separate economic events.
