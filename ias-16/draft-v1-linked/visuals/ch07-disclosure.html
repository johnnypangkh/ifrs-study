From Derecognition

7.0Disclosure

Required for each class

Class continuum — same grain as the entity’s Models classes (see Models).

Components for depreciation are not the same as disclosure classes.

7.1 Bases, methods, useful lives
  • Measurement bases
  • Depreciation methods
  • Useful lives or depreciation rates

5.3Land is generally not depreciated, so there is no depreciation method or useful life. Still disclose the measurement basis and the gross carrying amount.

7.2 Gross carrying amount and accumulated depreciation
  • Gross carrying amount at the beginning and end of the period
  • Accumulated depreciation aggregated with accumulated impairment losses, at the beginning and end
7.3 Reconciliation
  • Additions
  • Assets classified as held for sale or a disposal group, and other disposals
  • Acquisitions through business combinations
  • Increases or decreases from revaluations, and impairment recognised or reversed in other comprehensive income
  • Impairment losses recognised in profit or loss
  • Impairment losses reversed in profit or loss
  • Depreciation
  • Net exchange differences
  • Other changes

Split additions · present disposals as a single line when useful for users.

Held for sale / disposal group — IFRS 5

Also required
7.4 Restrictions, construction expenditure, commitments
  • Restrictions on title, and PPE pledged as security
  • Expenditure recognised in the carrying amount of assets in the course of construction
  • Contractual commitments for the acquisition of PPE
Depreciation and accumulated depreciation
  • Depreciation for the period, whether in profit or loss or in the cost of other assets
  • Accumulated depreciation at the end of the period
Changes in estimates
  • Changes in estimates: residual values, dismantling costs, useful lives, and depreciation methods (IAS 8)
7.5 Required if not presented separately
  • Compensation from third parties included in profit or loss
  • Proceeds and cost of items produced while bringing the asset to that location and condition, and the line items

20A proceeds before intended use — measurement home on Initial measurement (see Initial).

Compensation — separate economic events (see Derecognition / Compensation).

7.6 Required if stated at revalued amounts
  • In addition to IFRS 13
  • Effective date of the revaluation
  • Whether an independent valuer was involved
  • For each revalued class, the carrying amount under the cost model
  • Revaluation surplus, the change for the period, and any restrictions on distribution to shareholders

Fair value disclosures — IFRS 13

7.7 Encouraged
  • Temporarily idle carrying amount
  • Gross carrying amount of fully depreciated assets still in use
  • Carrying amount of assets retired from active use and not classified as held for sale
  • Fair value of PPE when the cost model is used, if materially different from carrying amount

Temporarily idle — depreciation continues until held for sale or derecognition (see Depreciation Continues).

7.3See also IAS 36 for impaired PPE

7.0See also IAS 23

Presentation of the PPE line — IAS 1.

Right-of-use asset presentation — IFRS 16.
