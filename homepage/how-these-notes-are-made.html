How these notes are made

These notes are a personal study pack for technical accountants — not a legal reprint and not advice. Each standard pack is built so you can study without reopening every Official and firm PDF for the points the pack covers: a Concept Map as the jump board, and a detail-dense teaching body under it. Claims stay tied to the Official Standard and to firm works that were actually used for that pack.

Source hierarchy

Official is primary. Firm manuals densify. FAQs and alerts only support a manual view.

1 Official Standard

IFRS / IAS text for that pack, including Basis for Conclusions and Illustrative Examples where the chapter uses them.

Rule: Requirements and definitions stay tied to Official locators.

2 Firm manuals actually used

PwC, Deloitte / DTT, EY, KPMG where on disk and scanned for the chapter.

Rule: A firm that was not used is not listed as if it were.

3 Firm FAQs / alerts

Support that densifies a manual view.

Rule: Never a substitute for Official text or for a manual that was never opened.

Every pack lists its real sources on the References page. Cite chips in the chapter point back to those same works.

Claim discipline

How claims stay honest before they stay on the page.

Official vs firm
Requirements and definitions stay tied to Official locators. Firm views, examples, and FAQs keep their own house tags — they are not rewritten as if they were the Standard.
Sources differ
When sources treat one point differently, the page shows both side by side and does not reconcile them into a third “in practice” answer.
No invent
Case facts and numbers come from Official or pack Big4 examples. Invented companies, CU amounts, requirements, or unsupported tips are not kept.
Cite chips
On every sourced point: firm (or Official) + work + locator inside the chip — no bare house name, no § in chips.
Traceability, not omniscience
A cite must match a source that was actually used for that pack. The page promises every teaching claim can be walked back to Official or a named firm work — not that every PDF on earth was scanned.
Thin ≠ done
Structure alone is not enough: placeholders that only restate a heading are not treated as finished teaching.

Surfaces on a chapter page

Equal-rank things the reader meets — a collection, not a procedure.

Concept Map

Teaching story in space and jump board into the chapter. One glance should show how the parts relate; section chips jump to the matching body section. The map does not re-teach the full text.

Sections

Deep study surface: definitions, tests, tables, callouts in technical IFRS wording — detail-first so the reader does not reopen the PDFs for every point the pack covers.

Cite chips

Source attribution on every sourced point (firm/Official + work + locator).

Illustrations

Worked examples from Official or pack Big4 named examples / case facts (Facts → Assessment / Conclusion). Plain firm prose without case facts stays ordinary teaching text, not an Illustration.

Pack chrome

Abbreviations (short forms used in that standard), References, bottom Disclaimer, and Last update on every pack page.
