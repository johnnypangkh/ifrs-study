Four FAIL tests for the left rail and kit 25. The three building blocks stay the kit. Abstract labels only — Phase, Peer, Outer, Start, End, marker.

1. Logic. A beat that needs a name has a left-rail cell. That cell’s border covers the beat. A phase has one kit 25 axis, and events of that phase sit on it.

L1 on this page

The outer left rail of a beat (phase rail or first-column section rail). Atlas .m-l1 plate fill is a different rule and stays as signed.

1

Missing grid

Every right-hand beat that needs a name has its own left-rail cell. That is each peer, each section, the phase, and each nest row.

FAIL

The detail is drawn and the left cell is missing, empty, or collapsed — or two named beats share one rail.

PASS

Each of those beats has its own named .inner-rail. One phase rail covers the whole kit 25 pane: rings, markers, span, Begins / Ceases, and a See-also chip on the card it qualifies. Formula terms and fork arms are not rails.

Exception only when Johnny writes one sentence on that mock. An unsigned mock has no exception. “The head label counts” is not an exception.

2

Equal height, parent covers

The rail border and its detail border share a top and a bottom. The outer rail and the board that holds its children are that same pair.

FAIL

|Δtop|, |Δbottom|, or |Δheight| greater than 2px. A parent only as tall as its label fails. A parent taller than its children by more than its own padding fails.

PASS

The two border boxes match within 2px. Signed Dep: During use matches the timeline pane, and Useful life matches its nest board. Words may sit centered inside a rail that already stretches.

Cite visual-grammar Alignment and Layers. This test is now on the MF page.

3

Markers on the axis

One phase pane has one kit 25 axis, from Start to End. Events of this phase sit on that line: one mark, one leader, one label.

PASS

Rings, span, and on-axis markers. Start / End cards belong to this axis. A different concept gets its own rail row. On Dep, year-end review is that rail, so Dep’s timeline has no year-end marks.

FAIL · second band

A legend plus a tick row in its own box under the kit 25 ends, inside the same pane. Reval’s ◆ row under kit 25 is this pattern. The Year-end band Dep dropped is this pattern. Live Impairment’s band under the axis is this pattern.

Dropping Reval’s dates, or drawing them on the axis, both pass. The band fails either way. That content pick stays with Johnny (timeline audit P1 / P2). This page does not redraw Reval or Dep.

4

L1 short stub

Yes. A short outer rail or phase rail that does not cover its beat fails. Same 2px test as rule 2.

FAIL

The phase name is a text-height label beside a tall kit 25 pane. Live .lc-head-k does this with align-self: end. An outer rail one line tall beside a nest that continues below it fails the same way.

PASS

The rail border covers the beat. Dep During use stretched to the timeline pane is the control. Reval’s L1 stretch stays; shrinking it fails. A nested peer that matches its own short detail passes. A dropped plate title is not a stub.
