Johnny: a timeline label is a flag punched on the floor, and a timeline is an arrow.
Punch5 had the right words and no second band, but「Revaluation date」floated above the
line, and Begins / Ceases hung below a plain line.

Grammar used (existing, not new chrome)
- Visual-grammar §6: ring on the axis, 2px pole, card on the pole; arrowhead on the axis;
  snapshot above the line; the span (what happens during) under the line.
- Pole width = --stroke-2 (2px). Arrowhead = the IFRS 18 .tr-tl arrow (--rail-bar-l).
- Line colour stays --muted, same as punch5.

What changed (both During-use timelines)
- The axis is a line that ends in an arrowhead. Time runs left → right.
- Begins is a flag: the pole's foot is on the start ring, the card stands on the pole
  and extends to the right.
- Ceases is a flag: the pole's foot is on the end ring, just before the arrowhead.
  The card extends back along the axis so it stays inside the frame.
  A card past the arrowhead would leave the pane.
- The span stays under the line, dashed, one line of the live words.
- Dep words:「Depreciate over useful life」, Begins, Ceases, See also - IFRS 5.
- Reval words:「Revalue during useful life」. The two live ◆ stay at 35% and 70%.
  「Revaluation date」is one flag. Its pole is planted on the axis at the midpoint
  of those two ◆. No .reval-date-row. No second copy of the words.

What stayed
- No second ◆ band (punch5).
- Reval punch4 Impairment reversal L2 rail | detail.
- Reval punch3: L1 stretch, no plate-title, other L2, solid L0, B11, rev-nest width.
- No live site file. No new IFRS wording.

- Pole foot = line top (gap 0) on Begins, Revaluation date, and Ceases.
- Card bottom = pole top. The card stands on the pole.
- Arrow starts where the line ends (line right 1155.8, arrow left 1155.8).
- Rings at 417.4 and 1147.8. Poles at 425.4 and 1139.8 (about 1px off the ring edge).
- ◆ centres 677.9 and 938.5 (35% and 70%). Date pole 808.2 = their midpoint.
- Date card 740–877, between the diamonds. Ceases card 969–1148, clear of the 70% ◆.
