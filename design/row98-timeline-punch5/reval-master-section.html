Impairment reversal
Impairment reversal: the carrying amount cannot rise above what it would have been, net of depreciation, had no impairment loss been recognised. On a revalued asset the reversal is treated as a revaluation increase - P&L to the extent it reverses an impairment loss previously recognised in P&L, the rest to OCI.
