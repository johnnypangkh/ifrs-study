## 1. Why IFRS 18 (teaching paragraph)

Investors asked for a **structured profit or loss** they can compare across entities: clear **categories** for income and expenses, defined **totals/subtotals**, disciplined **aggregation/disaggregation** between primary statements and notes, and audited disclosure of **management-defined performance measures (MPMs)** that previously lived only outside the FS. IFRS 18 answers that demand and **replaces IAS 1** for presentation/disclosure teaching from periods beginning on/after **1 Jan 2027** (earlier application permitted). Net profit often stays numerically familiar; the **story of the P&L** does not.

### Ch1 — What’s new vs IAS 1

- **Teaching goal:** CPA-level delta: what IAS 1 did **not** prescribe vs IFRS 18’s three new requirement sets; what carries forward vs what moved (e.g. IAS 8 rename/move — one-liner only).
- **Key § refs:** IN reasons (paraphrase) · Objective §1 · supersession context (Intro) · App C §C1.
- **Suggested VISUAL TYPE(s):** **Two-column contrast cards** (+ small **timeline** milestone for 2027).
- **Key visual ideas:** (1) IAS 1 diversity → IFRS 18 structure (categories / defined subtotals / MPM note / aggregation guidance). (2) “Net profit may look familiar; P&L **shape** changes” callout.
- **Traps:** Do not rebuild an IAS 1 pack; Deloitte A4 = bridge facts only; no long IN paste.

---

### Ch2 — PFS roles & aggregation

- **Teaching goal:** Primary FS = **useful structured summaries**; notes = material detail / supplement; aggregation shares characteristics, disaggregation separates dissimilar ones; labelling (“other” discipline); offsetting thin.
- **Key § refs:** §15–24 (roles · structured summary · additional line items) · §41–45 (aggregation / offsetting) · App B §B16–B26 (characteristics / “other”).
- **Suggested VISUAL TYPE(s):** **Concept map** (PFS ∥ Notes roles) + **checklist table** (aggregate vs disaggregate tests).
- **Key visual ideas:** (1) Dual-role plate: PFS “structured summary” ↔ Notes “material detail” with §16 / §17 chips. (2) Shared-characteristics cluster → line item; dissimilar → split (B17-style schematic, original drawing).
- **Traps:** Don’t turn aggregation into a fake process flowchart; don’t invent pipelines beyond §41 order; dashed only for see-also.

---

### Ch3 — P&L categories

- **Teaching goal:** Classify **all** income/expenses in P&L into five categories; operating as **default**; investing / financing logic (incl. specified main-business-activity overrides at overview depth); income taxes; discontinued; FX/derivatives classification cues thin.
- **Key § refs:** §47–68 · especially §47–49 · §52 (operating default) · §53–58 investing · §59–66 financing · §67 income taxes · discontinued via §47 / IFRS 5 bridge · App B §B29–B72 (classification AG — teach selectively).
- **Suggested VISUAL TYPE(s):** **Statement layout** (schematic P&L with **category colour bands**) + nested **decision tree** for “which category?” (main path only).
- **Key visual ideas:** (1) Full-height schematic P&L: five coloured category bands + placeholders for required subtotals (link to Ch4). (2) Compact decision: main business investing/financing? → operating override path (overview only — banks deep dive later).
- **Traps:** Don’t dump full bank/insurer AG; don’t treat “operating” as “recurring only” (IN/§: operating is default and complete); no rainbow without legend meanings.

---

### Ch4 — Totals & subtotals

- **Teaching goal:** Required presentation of **operating profit or loss**, **profit or loss before financing and income taxes** (with §73 policy exception awareness), and **profit or loss**; distinguish **IFRS-required / §118-listed** subtotals from **MPMs** (bridge to Ch6).
- **Key § refs:** §69–74 · §75–76 (line items / attribution) · §118 (listed non-MPM subtotals) · §24 (additional subtotals compatible with structure).
- **Suggested VISUAL TYPE(s):** **Statement layout** (brackets on category bands) + **matrix** (required vs §118-listed vs MPM).
- **Key visual ideas:** (1) Same P&L skeleton as Ch3 with bold brackets: Operating profit · Profit before financing & tax · Profit or loss. (2) Three-column matrix: Required (§69) | Listed §118 (not MPM) | MPM (→ Ch6) — colour contrast **IFRS subtotal token** vs **MPM token**.
- **Traps:** Don’t equate “gross profit” with MPM; don’t omit §73 special-entity path as a trap callout; no full IE reprint.

---

### Ch5 — Operating expenses

- **Teaching goal:** Inside **operating** category, present the most useful structured summary by **nature**, **function**, or **mixed**; function presentation triggers cost-of-sales line (when applicable) + single-note nature totals (§83).
- **Key § refs:** §78–85 · App B §B78–B85.
- **Suggested VISUAL TYPE(s):** **Matrix** (nature ∥ function ∥ mixed) + small **statement layout** slice of operating band.
- **Key visual ideas:** (1) Three-option matrix with “most useful structured summary” as selection cue (§78). (2) Function path → dashed see-also? **No** — use solid containment for the §83 single-note plate listing depreciation / amortisation / employee benefits / impairment / inventory write-downs allocated to function lines.
- **Traps:** Mixed is allowed (§79 / B81) — don’t teach “pick one forever for every line”; don’t expand §84 exemption into a loophole essay.

---

### Ch6 — Management-defined performance measures (MPMs)

- **Teaching goal:** What **is** / **is not** an MPM; public-communications presumption; **single note**; calculation · reconciliation to most comparable IFRS/§118 measure · tax & NCI effects; change disclosures.
- **Key § refs:** §117–125 · §118 (exclusions) · App B §B113–B141 (selectively).
- **Suggested VISUAL TYPE(s):** **Decision tree** (is it an MPM?) + **waterfall / build-up** (MPM ← reconciling items → IFRS/§118 anchor).
- **Key visual ideas:** (1) Gate tree: subtotal of income/expenses? → used in public communications? → management view of entity-as-a-whole? → **not** §118 / not IFRS-required? → MPM. (2) Reconciliation waterfall with **MPM colour** on the measure and **IFRS-subtotal colour** on the anchor — same contrast grammar as Ch4 matrix.
- **Traps:** §118 listed items are **not** MPMs; don’t invent non-GAAP outside the Standard’s definition; no Big 4 FAQ dump as body prose.

---

### Ch7 — Other FS impacts (thin)

- **Teaching goal:** Consequential, widely applicable bridges only: SCF starting point / interest & dividends classification tightening; SFP presentation largely carried with useful-structured-summary lens; EPS additional-measure limits; interim MPM parity (pointer to Ch8).
- **Key § refs:** App D / consequential amendments pointers · teach via IAS 7 / IAS 33 / IAS 34 **as amended by IFRS 18** (cite IFRS 18 App D + amended §§ in teaching chips, not a second pack). Official §96–112 (SFP/SCE) only as “mostly carried” cues.
- **Suggested VISUAL TYPE(s):** **By-phase / see-also strip** + tiny **statement layout** (SCF operating start).
- **Key visual ideas:** (1) Three thin bridge cards: SCF · SFP · EPS. (2) “Operating profit → indirect SCF start” one-liner schematic.
- **Traps:** Do **not** grow this into a full IAS 7 or IAS 33 course; banks SCF quirks stay in later see-also.

---

### Ch8 — Transition & effective date

- **Teaching goal:** **1 Jan 2027** annual periods (earlier OK); retrospective application; comparative P&L reconciliations; first-year interim heading/subtotal and reconciliation rules.
- **Key § refs:** App C §C1–C7 (and related interim paragraphs as tagged).
- **Suggested VISUAL TYPE(s):** **Timeline** + **checklist table** (annual vs interim first-year duties).
- **Key visual ideas:** (1) Timeline: issue → prepare systems/MPM inventory → 1 Jan 2027 → first annual under IFRS 18. (2) Two-column checklist: Annual C3 reconciliation ∥ Interim C4–C5 duties.
- **Traps:** Don’t bury effective date only in footnotes; don’t require full historical IE walkthroughs.

## 4. Colour grammar proposal (IFRS 18)

Same **logic** as Johnny Ch2 consol decode: colour only when the legend (or on-node label) can state the teaching meaning in one plain line; same meaning → same token across the pack; peers that are not categorical stay neutral.

### Category bands (P&L statement layouts — Ch3 / Ch4 / Ch5 operating slice)

| Token (working name) | Meaning (legend one-liner) | Where |
|----------------------|----------------------------|--------|
| `--cat-operating` | Operating category (default) | Band + chips |
| `--cat-investing` | Investing category | Band + chips |
| `--cat-financing` | Financing category | Band + chips |
| `--cat-tax` | Income taxes category | Band + chips |
| `--cat-discontinued` | Discontinued operations category | Band + chips |

Neutral ink/surface for non-category chrome (titles, operators, § chips source class unchanged).

### MPM vs IFRS subtotals contrast (Ch4 matrix · Ch6 waterfall)

| Token | Meaning | Parallel to consol grammar |
|-------|---------|----------------------------|
| `--ifrs-subtotal` | IFRS-required or §118-listed subtotal (audited IFRS measure space) | Like **solid Parent / Unconsol FS** — “in the Standard’s measure set” |
| `--mpm` | Management-defined performance measure (single-note reconciliation world) | Like **dashed Consol plate** contrast — **different regime**, not “more important colour” |

**Do not** paint every §118 row a different hue. Two tokens only for the contrast. Required §69 brackets can share `--ifrs-subtotal` with a weight/bracket treatment difference (line weight / label), not a third rainbow.

### Dashed (rare — unchanged site law)

- See-also bridges/chips (e.g. Banks/insurers later; IAS 7 bridge).
- Matrix N/A cells if any.
- Consol group plate **only** if a consol relationship is taught (unlikely in v1 IFRS 18 core).
- **Not** for nesting MPM under P&L — use containment or the MPM token instead.

### Legend

Right-rail only when bands or MPM contrast appear; skip if every band is already labelled on the schematic.
