3.1Statement of profit or loss — five categories

3.2 Operating default

Everything not classified in the other four — a complete operations picture, not “recurring only”

3.3 Investing

Returns from associates, JVs and unconsolidated subsidiaries · cash & equivalents · other stand-alone-return assets

3.4 Financing

Liabilities that only raise finance · specified interest-like amounts on other liabilities

3.5Specified main business activities

Only two: investing in assets · providing financing to customers. Where one applies, some amounts that would sit in Investing or Financing are classified in Operating instead.

3.6 Income taxes

Specified income-tax amounts

3.6 Discontinued operations

IFRS 5 bridge — discontinued-operations amounts kept apart from continuing

4.1Ch4 adds required subtotals across these bands — operating profit or loss · profit or loss before financing and income taxes. They are totals, not extra categories.
