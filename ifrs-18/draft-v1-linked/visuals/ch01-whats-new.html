1.1IAS 1-era gaps vs IFRS 18 answer

IAS 1-era gaps

  • Limited rules on P&L classification
  • Undefined subtotals above profit or loss
  • Weak grouping / aggregation guidance

Diversity investors criticised

IFRS 18 answer

  • Categories + defined subtotals
  • Aggregation / PFS–notes roles
  • MPM single-note regime

Transparent, comparable performance

Familiar net profit ≠ unchanged story. Bottom-line total can stay stable while categories, required subtotals, and the MPM note rearrange the P&L shape.
