2.1Roles — where information lives

PFS — useful structured summary

Overview · compare entities/periods · flag where to dig in notes

  • More aggregated line items
  • Structure differs by statement (P&L: categories + subtotals)

Notes — material detail / supplement

Explain PFS lines · add what a summary cannot carry

  • More detailed / disaggregated information

Roles decide where info goes — not two unrelated dumps · both carry material information only

2.3Aggregation & disaggregation principles

Aggregation test
Ask
Outcome
Shared characteristics?

Do items share enough to aggregate into one line?

Candidate aggregate line

Dissimilar?

Would separating dissimilar characteristics give material information?

Disaggregate (PFS or notes)

PFS role?

Does the line help a useful structured summary?

Keep / shape the PFS line

Notes role?

Is further detail needed to understand or supplement?

Disclose in notes

Obscuring?

Does the grouping hide material information?

Stop / split — do not obscure

Trap: Aggregation is not a fixed pipeline; apply it through shared characteristics, roles and no obscuring.
