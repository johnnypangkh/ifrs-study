5.1 How the face can present OpEx

Nature

By resource consumed; no allocation to activities.

e.g. Raw materials · Employee benefits · Depreciation

Function

By activity; one line can hold several natures.

e.g. Cost of sales · Distribution · Administrative

Mixed

One basis per line; lines can differ.

e.g. Cost of sales (function) + Depreciation (nature)

Trap: “One basis for the whole operating band.” Mixed is allowed — one basis per line — and a single function line is enough to require the nature totals note.
