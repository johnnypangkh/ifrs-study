Three nested terms; only the innermost changes a classification

Operations → Operating category (residual)

Everything not classified elsewhere, however volatile or unusual

Main business activities — any, e.g. manufacturing, retail, services

No test needed: their results land in operating anyway, except equity-accounted results

Specified main business activities — only two are tested

Investing in assets

e.g. investment property company, insurer, investment entity

Providing financing to customers

e.g. bank, lender, manufacturer that finances buyers

Outside operations

Investing · financing · income taxes · discontinued

Why only these two?

Their results would otherwise leave operating profit
