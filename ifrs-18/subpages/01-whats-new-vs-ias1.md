## Teaching sections

### 1.0 Scope of this chapter `{#s-1-0}`

IFRS 18 answers investor demand for a structured profit or loss they can compare across entities. This chapter is the CPA-level delta vs IAS 1: what IAS 1 did not prescribe in detail, and the three new requirement sets IFRS 18 adds. Site labels stay IFRS 18 only. Effective for annual periods beginning on or after 1 Jan 2027 (earlier application permitted). **§1**, **App C §C1**, **IN4–IN7**

> **Pitfall:** Rebuilding an IAS 1 pack under a new cover. Deloitte IAS 1 A4 = bridge facts only — do not structure teaching as IAS 1. **IN4–IN6**

### 1.1 Why IFRS 18 — investor demand for comparable performance `{#s-1-1}`

Under IAS 1-era practice, Standards did not spell out in detail: (a) where to classify income and expenses in P&L; (b) which subtotals to present above profit or loss; or (c) how to group information between primary statements and notes. Diversity followed — entities defined their own subtotals and grouping habits, which investors said made analysis and comparison hard. **IN4–IN5**

IFRS 18 responds with more transparent, comparable performance information. Net profit often stays numerically familiar; the story of the P&L (categories, defined subtotals, aggregation discipline, MPM note) does not. **IN6–IN7**

**Contrast**

| IAS 1-era gap | IFRS 18 answer |
|---|---|
| Thin rules on P&L classification · undefined subtotals above profit · weak grouping/aggregation guidance → diversity that investors criticised. **IN4–IN5** | Categories + defined subtotals · aggregation / PFS–notes roles · MPM single-note regime. **IN7–IN8**, **IN14–IN16** (paraphrase) |

### 1.2 Three new requirement sets `{#s-1-2}`

Teach the delta as three sets (not a laundry list of line moves):

| Set | Teaching paraphrase | Where in this pack | Cite |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Structured P&L | Classify income/expenses into defined categories; present operating profit and (subject to §73) profit before financing and income taxes | Ch3 · Ch4 | **IN8–IN11**, **§47**, **§69** |
| 2. Grouping discipline | PFS = useful structured summaries; notes = material detail / supplement; aggregation & labelling (“other”) rules | Ch2 | **§15–24**, **§41–45** |
| 3. MPM disclosures | Management-defined performance measures used in public communications enter a single audited note with reconciliations | Ch6 | **§117–125** |

> **Concept insight:** Operating is the default category — a complete picture of operations for the period, including volatile / non-recurring items that are not classified elsewhere. Do not teach “operating = recurring only.” **IN9**, **§52**

### 1.3 What carries / what moves (thin) `{#s-1-3}`

- Recognition & measurement stay in other Standards — IFRS 18 is presentation and disclosure. **§4**

- Complete set / going concern / consistency / comparatives largely continue in updated form (teach when needed; do not rebuild IAS 1).

- IAS 8 absorbs some former IAS 1 topics (accounting policies / estimates / errors) — one-liner only here; not a second pack.

- SCF remains primarily IAS 7, amended consequentially — thin bridge in Ch7. **§3**

#### Worked example — familiar bottom line, new shape

- **Facts:** Entity’s “profit or loss” total is unchanged in amount after reclassification under IFRS 18.
- **Concept / gate:** Bottom-line total can be stable while categories, required subtotals, and MPM note rearrange the story. **§69–72**, **IN6**
- **Conclusion:** Familiar net profit ≠ unchanged P&L teaching. Train the shape (bands + brackets + MPM), not only the total.

### 1.4 Effective date cue `{#s-1-4}`

Apply for annual reporting periods beginning on or after 1 Jan 2027; earlier application permitted (disclose if early). Transition detail → Ch8. **App C §C1**

> See also: Banks / insurers main-business-activity overrides — Map dashed chip (later chapter); overview only in Ch3. **§49–51**, **§55–58**, **§65–66**
