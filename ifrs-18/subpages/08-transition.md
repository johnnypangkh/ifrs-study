## Teaching sections

### 8.0 Scope of this chapter `{#s-8-0}`

Close the pack on when and how: effective date, retrospective application, comparative P&L reconciliations, and first-year interim duties. **App C §C1–C7**

> **Pitfall:** Burying 1 Jan 2027 only in a footnote. Put the date on the timeline and in the opening teaching line. **§C1**

### 8.1 Effective date `{#s-8-1}`

Apply IFRS 18 for annual reporting periods beginning on or after 1 January 2027. Earlier application is permitted — disclose that fact if early. **§C1**

This Standard supersedes IAS 1 (HKAS 1 in the official HK text; site label remains IFRS 18 / IAS 1). **§C8**

### 8.2 Retrospective application `{#s-8-2}`

Apply retrospectively under IAS 8. Quantitative IAS 8 §28(f)-style disclosures are not required on initial application of IFRS 18. **§C2**

### 8.3 Annual reconciliations (first IFRS 18 annual) `{#s-8-3}`

In the first annual FS under IFRS 18, disclose for the comparative period immediately preceding first application a reconciliation for each P&L line item between: **§C3**

- restated amounts applying IFRS 18; and

- amounts previously presented applying IAS 1.

Reconciliations for the current period or earlier comparatives are permitted but not required. **§C6**

### 8.4 Interim — first year of application `{#s-8-4}`

| Duty | Teaching paraphrase | Cite |
|---|---|---|
| Headings & subtotals | In condensed interims in the first year, present each heading expected under IFRS 18 and the §69–74 subtotals — do not rely on IAS 34 §10 relief for headings/subtotals until the first annual IFRS 18 FS is issued | **§C4** |
| Reconciliations | As part of IAS 34 change disclosures, reconcile each P&L line for comparative periods (current + cumulative) between IFRS 18 policies and previous IAS 1 policies | **§C5** |

#### Worked example — calendar-year adopter

- **Facts:** 31 Dec year-end; first annual period beginning 1 Jan 2027; issues Q1 2027 condensed interim.
- **Concept / gate:** Q1 2027 interim already shows IFRS 18 headings/subtotals (§C4) and comparative reconciliations (§C5); 2027 annual carries §C3 reconciliation for 2026 comparatives.
- **Conclusion:** Systems & MPM inventory cannot wait for year-end 2027 — interim first year is live.

### 8.5 Optional election (thin) `{#s-8-5}`

At initial application, eligible entities may change the IAS 28 election for an associate/JV from equity method to FVTPL (IFRS 9), retrospectively — with a matching change in separate FS under IAS 27. Teaching awareness only. **§C7**

### 8.6 Timeline checklist `{#s-8-6}`

- Issue / prepare systems · category mapping · MPM inventory

- 1 Jan 2027 (or earlier if elected) — periods begin under IFRS 18

- First-year interims — headings/subtotals + reconciliations

- First annual — §C3 comparative P&L reconciliations
