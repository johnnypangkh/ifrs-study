## Teaching sections

### 6.0 Scope of this chapter `{#s-6-0}`

MPMs bring management’s performance story inside the audited FS via a single note — with calculation, reconciliation to the most comparable IFRS/§118 measure, and tax & NCI effects. This chapter is the definition gate, the exclusions, and the disclosure build-up. **§117–125**, **B113–B141** (selective)

> **Pitfall:** Treating every non-GAAP label as an MPM — or treating §118 items as MPMs. Run the definition; apply exclusions. **§117–118**

### 6.1 What is an MPM? `{#s-6-1}`

A management-defined performance measure is a subtotal of income and expenses that: **§117**

- the entity uses in public communications outside financial statements;

- the entity uses to communicate to users management’s view of an aspect of financial performance of the entity as a whole; and

- is not listed in §118 and is not specifically required to be presented or disclosed by IFRS Accounting Standards.

| Gate | Ask | If no → |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Subtotal of income and expenses? | Outside MPM definition (other metrics may still exist — different regime) |
| 2 | Used in public communications outside FS? | Not MPM under §117(a) |
| 3 | Communicates management’s view of performance of the entity as a whole? (presumption in §119) | May rebut only with reasonable & supportable information (§120) |
| 4 | Not §118-listed and not IFRS-required? | If it is §118 / required → not MPM |

> **Concept insight:** Design decision tree should follow these gates — then hand off to the waterfall for measures that pass. **§117**, **§119–120**

### 6.2 Public-communications presumption `{#s-6-2}`

Presume that a subtotal of income/expenses used in public communications outside the FS communicates management’s view of an aspect of entity-as-a-whole performance — unless the entity rebuts under §120 with reasonable and supportable information. **§119–120**, **B124–B131**

### 6.3 §118 exclusions — not MPMs `{#s-6-3}`

Repeat the Ch4 matrix exclusions as a hard stop: gross profit (and similar); operating profit before D&A and IAS 36 impairments; operating profit + equity-method investment results; §73-path operating+investing subtotal; profit before tax; profit from continuing operations. **§118**

**Contrast**

| IFRS / §118 measure space | MPM regime |
|---|---|
| Required §69 brackets and §118-listed subtotals — audited IFRS measure set. Token: --ifrs-subtotal. **§69**, **§118** | Passes §117 → single note, reconciliation, tax & NCI effects. Token: --mpm. Different regime — not “brighter importance colour.” **§117**, **§122–123** |

### 6.4 Single note — disclosure objective & contents `{#s-6-4}`

Objective: help users understand (a) which aspect of performance management thinks the MPM communicates, and (b) how it compares with IFRS-defined measures. **§121**

Disclose all MPMs in a single note, including a statement that MPMs reflect management’s view and are not necessarily comparable with similarly labelled measures of other entities. **§122**

For each MPM, disclose: **§123**

- description of the aspect of performance communicated (and why management thinks it is useful);

- how the MPM is calculated;

- a reconciliation to the most directly comparable §118-listed subtotal or IFRS-required total/subtotal;

- income-tax effect and NCI effect for each reconciling item;

- how the tax effect was determined (B141).

> **Concept insight:** Waterfall visual: MPM (mpm token) ← reconciling items → IFRS/§118 anchor (ifrs-subtotal token). Same contrast grammar as Ch4 matrix. **§123(c)–(d)**

#### Worked example — “adjusted operating profit” waterfall

- **Facts:** Earnings release highlights “adjusted operating profit” = operating profit excluding a material restructuring expense; used in investor deck.
- **Concept / gate:** Subtotal of income/expenses · public communications · management view · not §118 → MPM. Reconcile to operating profit (§69/§70); show tax & NCI on the reconciling item. **§117**, **§123**
- **Conclusion:** Single note + reconciliation — do not leave the bridge only in unaudited slides.

### 6.5 Changes to MPMs `{#s-6-5}`

If calculation changes, a new MPM is added, a previous MPM ceases, or tax-effect methodology changes: explain the change and effects; give reasons; restate comparatives unless impracticable (apply IAS 8 impracticability ideas; selecting an MPM is not an accounting-policy choice). If comparatives are omitted as impracticable, say so. **§124–125**

### 6.6 Optional teaching prompts (gate order) `{#s-6-6}`

- Income/expense subtotal? → public communications? → management view of entity-as-a-whole (presumption)? → not §118 / not IFRS-required? → MPM. **§117–120**

- If MPM → single note · calculate · reconcile · tax & NCI. **§122–123**

- If change → explain · reasons · restate (or impracticable disclosure). **§124–125**
