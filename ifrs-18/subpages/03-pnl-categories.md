## Teaching sections

### 3.0 Scope of this chapter `{#s-3-0}`

Classify all income and expenses included in profit or loss into five categories. Operating is the default. Investing and financing follow specified asset/liability logic, with overview-depth overrides when investing in assets or providing financing to customers is a main business activity. Income taxes and discontinued operations close the set. FX / derivatives classification cues stay thin. **§47–68**, **B29–B72** (selective)

> **Pitfall:** Teaching “operating = recurring only.” Operating is residual/default and gives a complete picture of operations for the period — including volatile items not classified elsewhere. **§52**, **IN9**

### 3.1 Five categories — the P&L bands `{#s-3-1}`

An entity shall classify income and expenses included in the statement of profit or loss in one of five categories: **§47**

| Category | Token (Design) | One-line meaning | Cite |
|---|---|---|---|
| Operating | --cat-operating | Default — everything not in the other four | **§52** |
| Investing | --cat-investing | Returns from specified assets (associates/JVs/unconsol. subsidiaries; cash & equivalents; other stand-alone-return assets) — subject to main-business overrides | **§53–58** |
| Financing | --cat-financing | Income/expenses from raising-finance liabilities and specified interest-like amounts on other liabilities — subject to main-business overrides | **§59–66** |
| Income taxes | --cat-tax | Tax amounts classified in this category | **§67** |
| Discontinued operations | --cat-discontinued | Discontinued-operations amounts (IFRS 5 bridge) | **§68** |

§48 points to App B for FX differences, net monetary position gains/losses, and derivatives / designated hedges — teach as “classify with the related item’s category” at overview depth; do not dump full AG. **§48**, **B65–B76**

> **Concept insight:** Design’s statement-layout schematic should show five coloured bands with placeholders for Ch4 required subtotals (brackets land in Ch4). Colour only with legend meaning. **§47**, **§69**

### 3.2 Operating — the default category `{#s-3-2}`

Classify in operating all income and expenses in P&L that are not classified in investing, financing, income taxes, or discontinued operations. **§52**, **B42**

- Includes income and expenses from main business activities — but is not limited to them.

- Includes volatile / unusual items that do not belong in another category.

- Operating profit (Ch4) comprises everything in this band. **§70**

**Contrast**

| What it is — default / residual | Common trap — “recurring only” |
|---|---|
| Complete operations picture for the period: whatever is not classified elsewhere. **§52**, **IN9** | Filtering “non-recurring” out of operating invents a category IFRS 18 does not define. Use MPM note (Ch6) for management views — not a fake operating filter. **§52**, **§117** |

### 3.3 Investing category `{#s-3-3}`

Except for specified main-business-activity rules (§55–58), classify in investing the §54 income/expenses from: **§53**

- investments in associates, joint ventures and unconsolidated subsidiaries;

- cash and cash equivalents; and

- other assets that generate a return individually and largely independently of the entity’s other resources.

§54 amounts include: income generated by those assets; measurement / derecognition income and expenses; and incremental expenses directly attributable to acquiring/disposing of them (e.g. transaction costs, costs to sell). **§54**, **B47**

> **Concept insight:** Investing separates stand-alone investment returns from the operating story — so users can analyse them apart from operations. **IN10**

### 3.4 Financing category `{#s-3-4}`

Distinguish liabilities that arise from transactions involving only the raising of finance from other liabilities. **§59**

| Liability type | What goes to financing (overview) | Cite |
|---|---|---|
| Raising-finance only (e.g. bank loans, bonds) | Measurement / derecognition income & expenses on those liabilities + incremental issue/extinguishment costs — subject to §63–66 | **§60** |
| Other liabilities (e.g. leases, pensions — not “only raising finance”) | Interest income/expense and interest-rate-change income/expense only if identified for applying other IFRS requirements | **§61** |

Derivatives / designated hedges follow B70–B76 (not the §60–61 path). Certain investment-contract and IFRS 17 insurance finance amounts are excluded from financing and classified in operating. **§63–64**

### 3.5 Main business activity — overview gate (not bank deep dive) `{#s-3-5}`

To classify operating / investing / financing, assess whether the entity has a specified main business activity: investing in particular assets, or providing financing to customers. **§49**, **B30–B41**

If yes, some income/expenses that would otherwise sit in investing or financing are classified in operating instead (§55–58, §65–66). Disclose the fact of such a main business activity (and changes in assessment). **§50–51**

> See also (later): Banks / insurers deep dive — Map dashed chip only in v1. Do not expand full AG here. **§55–58**, **§65–66**

#### Worked example — main-path classification (non-bank)

- **Facts:** Manufacturer: (1) revenue & COGS; (2) interest on surplus cash; (3) interest on bank loan; (4) income tax expense; (5) IFRS 5 discontinued result.
- **Concept / gate:** (1) operating default · (2) investing (cash & equivalents) · (3) financing (raising-finance liability) · (4) tax category · (5) discontinued. **§52–54**, **§59–60**, **§67–68**
- **Conclusion:** Ask “which category?” before inventing management labels. MPM views come later (Ch6), after IFRS bands exist.

### 3.6 Income taxes & discontinued operations `{#s-3-6}`

Classify specified income-tax amounts in the income taxes category. **§67**

Classify discontinued-operations amounts in the discontinued operations category (IFRS 5 presentation bridge — single amount still appears among required line items in Ch4). **§68**, **§75(a)(v)**

### 3.7 Optional teaching prompts (not a Board flowchart) `{#s-3-7}`

> Label: Pedagogy order for the main path — not an invented regulatory diagnostic beyond §47–68.

- Is the amount in P&L income/expense? → must sit in one of five categories. **§47**

- Discontinued? → discontinued category. **§68**

- Income taxes (as specified)? → tax category. **§67**

- Investing-asset return (§53–54), after main-business overrides? → investing. **§53–58**

- Financing-liability amounts (§59–61), after overrides? → financing. **§59–66**

- Else → operating default. **§52**
