## Teaching sections

### 2.0 Scope of this chapter `{#s-2-0}`

Before category mechanics, lock where information lives: primary financial statements (PFS) provide useful structured summaries; notes provide material detail and supplements. Aggregation / disaggregation and faithful labelling (“other” discipline) serve those roles. **§15–24**, **§41–45**, **B16–B26**

> **Pitfall:** Turning aggregation into a fake process flowchart. Teach principles in §41 order — shared characteristics → roles → no obscuring — not an invented pipeline. **§41**

### 2.1 Roles — PFS vs notes `{#s-2-1}`

To achieve the objective of financial statements, an entity presents information in the PFS and discloses information in the notes — but only material information. **§15**, **§19**

| Role | Teaching paraphrase | Cite |
|---|---|---|
| PFS | Structured summaries of recognised assets, liabilities, equity, income, expenses and cash flows — useful for overview, comparisons, and spotting where to dig in notes | **§16** |
| Notes | Material information to understand PFS line items and to supplement PFS so the FS objective is achieved | **§17** |
| Consequence | PFS information is more aggregated; notes carry more detailed / disaggregated information | **§18** |

Use the roles to decide where information goes. Specific Standards may require PFS presentation or note disclosure; immaterial required items need not be provided — even if listed as “minimum.” Additional disclosures may still be needed when specific requirements are insufficient. **§18–20**

**Contrast**

| PFS — useful structured summary | Notes — material detail / supplement |
|---|---|
| Overview · compare entities/periods · flag dig-deeper areas. Structure requirements differ by statement (P&L uses §47, §69, §76, §78). **§16**, **§21–22** | Explain line items · add information PFS cannot carry at summary level. **§17**, **§18(b)** |

### 2.2 Additional line items & subtotals on PFS `{#s-2-2}`

When needed for a useful structured summary, present additional line items and (compatible) subtotals — labelled so they faithfully represent the amounts included, and presented consistently. Additional P&L subtotals must be compatible with the category / required-subtotal structure. **§23–24**

### 2.3 Aggregation & disaggregation principles `{#s-2-3}`

Applying §41, an entity shall:

- aggregate items based on shared characteristics;

- disaggregate items based on characteristics that are not shared;

- aggregate/disaggregate so PFS line items fulfil the useful structured summary role;

- aggregate/disaggregate so notes fulfil the material information role;

- ensure aggregation/disaggregation does not obscure material information. **§41**, **B3**

Disaggregate whenever the resulting information is material. If material information is not presented on the PFS, disclose it in the notes. **§42**

| Checklist test | Ask | Cite |
|---|---|---|
| Shared characteristics? | Do items share enough characteristics to aggregate into one line? | **§41(a)** |
| Dissimilar? | Would separating dissimilar characteristics give material information? | **§41(b)**, **§42** |
| PFS role? | Does the line help a useful structured summary (overview / compare / dig cue)? | **§41(c)**, **§16** |
| Notes role? | Is further detail needed to understand or supplement? | **§41(d)**, **§17** |
| Obscuring? | Does the grouping hide material information? | **§41(e)** |

### 2.4 Labelling & “other” discipline `{#s-2-4}`

Label and describe totals, subtotals, line items and note items so they faithfully represent the characteristics of the item — including explanations of terms and how aggregation was done when needed. **§43**, **B24–B26**

> **Pitfall:** Dumping dissimilar leftovers into an unexplained “other” bucket that obscures material items. “Other” needs the same faithful-representation discipline as named lines. **§43**, **B16–B26**

#### Worked example — shared vs dissimilar

- **Facts:** One PFS line mixes routine staff costs with a material one-off restructuring severance that users would dig for.
- **Concept / gate:** Dissimilar characteristics + material information → disaggregate (PFS or notes). Roles decide where. **§41–42**, **§16–17**
- **Conclusion:** Don’t hide the dig cue. Keep PFS summary useful; put material dissimilar detail where roles require.

### 2.5 Offsetting (thin) `{#s-2-5}`

Do not offset assets and liabilities or income and expenses unless required or permitted by a Standard. Measuring assets net of valuation allowances is not offsetting. **§44–45**
