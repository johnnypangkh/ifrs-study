## Teaching sections

### 7.0 Scope of this chapter `{#s-7-0}`

Thin bridges only. IFRS 18’s consequential amendments touch SCF, SFP presentation cues, EPS additional-measure limits, and interim MPM parity — without turning this pack into IAS 7 / IAS 33 / IAS 34 courses. Cite IFRS 18 App D + amended Standards in chips; keep body short. **App D**, **§96–112** (SFP/SCE “mostly carried”)

> **Pitfall:** Growing Ch7 into a full cash-flow or EPS syllabus. Banks’ SCF quirks stay in the later see-also. **App D**

### 7.1 Statement of cash flows — operating profit start `{#s-7-1}`

IAS 7 is amended so the indirect method starts from operating profit or loss (the IFRS 18 subtotal), not from a looser “profit” starting point. Interest and dividends classification is tightened under the consequential amendments — teach as a one-line awareness cue, not a classification essay. **App D** (IAS 7 as amended)

> **Concept insight:** Tiny statement-layout stub: Operating profit → adjustments → operating cash flows. **§70** + IAS 7 bridge

### 7.2 Statement of financial position — carried with structured-summary lens `{#s-7-2}`

SFP line-item and current/non-current mechanics are largely carried forward, now read through the useful structured summary and aggregation roles (Ch2). Do not re-litigate the whole SFP here. **§96**, **§104**, **§15–24**, **§41–45**

### 7.3 EPS — additional-measure limits (thin) `{#s-7-3}`

IAS 33 consequential amendments constrain how additional per-share measures interact with IFRS 18’s performance measures — especially when an MPM is involved. Teaching cue: additional EPS-like measures must stay consistent with the Standard’s measure discipline; details live in amended IAS 33, not a second pack. **App D** (IAS 33 as amended)

### 7.4 Interim — MPM parity pointer `{#s-7-4}`

Aggregation and MPM requirements (§41–45, §117–125) apply to condensed interim FS. First-year interim heading/subtotal and reconciliation duties → Ch8. **§5**, **App C §C4–C5**

> See also: Banks / insurers SCF nuances — later chapter (Map dashed chip). IAS 7 / IAS 33 full courses — out of v1 scope.

#### Worked example — why SCF start matters

- **Facts:** Preparer previously started indirect SCF from “profit before tax,” mixing financing and tax into the bridge.
- **Concept / gate:** IFRS 18 operating profit is the defined operations subtotal — aligning SCF start improves comparability with the P&L bands. **§70**, App D / IAS 7
- **Conclusion:** Same operating story across P&L and SCF start — thin bridge, big teaching payoff.
