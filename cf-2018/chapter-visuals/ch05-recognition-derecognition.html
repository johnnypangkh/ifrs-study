5.3 Pre-2018 vs 2018 delta

Teaching note Old probability recognition criterion is removed / replaced by 2018 relevance + faithful representation filters. Do not teach “probable + reliably measurable” as CF 2018 recognition gates.
Pre-2018 memory

Old emphasis

Probability + reliable measurement taught as gates; “expected” flows in some definitions.

2018 CF filter

Removed / replaced

Relevance + faithful representation. Low probability may still be an A/L; recognition can still fail.

5.6 Derecognition — twin aims

Aim 1

What is retained

Faithfully represent what is retained after the transaction.

Aim 2

The change

Faithfully represent the change from the transaction.

both aims must be weighed — may align or conflict

What it means

Full transfer

No retained exposure → typically derecognise; both aims align.

Common trap

Partial · retained exposure

Aims may conflict — Standard may keep recognition, enhance disclosure, or other depiction.
