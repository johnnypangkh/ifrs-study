1.6 Accrual vs cash

Lens A

Accrual

Effects of transactions/events in the periods they occur — not only when cash moves.

Lens B

Cash flows

Liquidity / past cash generation & use — timing of cash itself.

Both

Complementary for assessing future net cash inflows — CF treats them as joint inputs, not an either/or.
