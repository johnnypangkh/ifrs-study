5.5Faithful representation

A faithful depiction of the item and of the related income, expenses or equity changes

Factors — not kill switches

Measurement uncertainty — reasonable estimates are essential

Related depiction — resulting income or expenses; recognition inconsistency

If strained

Uncertain estimate + explanation — may still be most useful

Next-best measure — slightly less relevant, lower uncertainty

No useful measure — limited cases: do not recognise

Cost constraint — benefits of recognition must justify its costs; consider what users would see if it is not recognised
