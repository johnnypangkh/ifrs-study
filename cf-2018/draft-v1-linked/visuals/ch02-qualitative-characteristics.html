2.1Qualitative characteristics of useful financial information

2.5Fundamental — both required

2.2Relevance

Capable of making a difference in users’ decisions — even if some users already know it

Capable via

Predictive value — an input to users’ own predictions; need not be a forecast

Confirmatory value — confirms or changes previous evaluations

Entity-specific

Materiality — omitting, misstating or obscuring it could reasonably be expected to influence primary users; nature and/or magnitude, no uniform threshold

+

2.3Faithful representation

Depicts the substance of the phenomenon, not merely its legal form

Maximise

Complete — everything needed to understand the phenomenon

Neutral — no bias in selection or presentation

Free from error — in description and process; not perfect accuracy

Judgement

Prudence — caution that supports neutrality; no over- or understatement either way

Measurement uncertainty — a reasonable, clearly described estimate can still be useful

Most relevant measure is a highly uncertain estimate? Trade off relevance against faithful representation

2.42018: substance over form and prudence reinstated inside faithful representation; the label does not revert to “reliability”

usefulness enhanced by — only for information already relevant and faithful; cannot rescue information that is not

2.6Enhancing — maximise, in no prescribed order

Comparability

Like things look alike, different things different; consistency helps, uniformity is not the goal

Verifiability

Independent, knowledgeable observers could reach consensus — not necessarily full agreement

Timeliness

Available in time to influence decisions; older information is generally less useful

Understandability

Classified, characterised and presented clearly; complex items are not left out

2.7Cost constraint

Pervasive — the benefits of reporting must justify its costs, for every characteristic

Providerscollect, process, verify, disseminate

Usersanalyse; obtain or estimate what is missing

Boardjudges benefits against costs for reporting generally, not entity by entity
