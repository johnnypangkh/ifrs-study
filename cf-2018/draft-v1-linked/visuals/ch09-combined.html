9.0

Combined financial statements

Reporting-entity label, not a Standard: two or more entities not all linked by parent–subsidiary · boundary set by faithful representation for primary users · the Framework does not say when or how to prepare them.

Label

9.1 Does the reporting entity comprise two or more entities that are not all linked by a parent–subsidiary relationship?

Yes
  • Combined financial statements
  • Boundary must still give relevant, faithful information
No
  • Parent and subsidiaries → consolidated; parent alone → unconsolidated 3.6
  • Portion of one entity → carve-out 10.1

Boundary

9.2 Is the set of economic activities complete and non-arbitrary, and does including it give neutral information?

Yes
  • Relevant, faithful information for primary users
  • Describe how the boundary was set and what constitutes the reporting entity
No
  • Faithful representation fails
  • Arbitrary or incomplete set — e.g. a portion without its share of overheads — can mislead

9.5 What binds the activities

Common control

  • Reporting entity for the period of common control
  • Owner B, three LLCs 9.5.3

Common management only

  • PwC Rarely appropriate 9.5.7
  • KPMG May be reasonable 9.5.4 9.5.5
  • Sources differ · not reconciled

Common business only

  • Seldom sufficient
  • Similar business, never controlled or managed together 9.5.6

9.6 Preparation approach

9.6.1Top-down

  • Extract amounts from the larger consolidated FS
  • IFRS 1 D16(a) 9.6.3

9.6.2Bottom-up

  • Build from component entity FS
  • D16(b) / continuity 9.6.4 – 9.6.6

9.7 Historical or pro forma

Historical

  • Actual past amounts, rational allocations
  • IFRS compliance can be claimed 9.7.1

Pro forma

  • “As-if” effects of events that did not occur
  • Does not comply with IFRS 9.7.2
