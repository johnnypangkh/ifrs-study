4.1Assets − Liabilities = Equity

4.2Asset

Present economic resource controlled by the entity as a result of past events

Right ↔ obligationpresent right with potential to produce benefits

Direction of benefitsflow in to the entity

Linking testcontrol — direct the use and obtain the benefits

−

4.3Liability

Present obligation of the entity to transfer an economic resource as a result of past events

Right ↔ obligationpresent obligation with potential to require a transfer

Direction of benefitsflow out of the entity

Linking testno practical ability to avoid the transfer

=

4.4Equity

Residual interest in the assets after deducting all liabilities
