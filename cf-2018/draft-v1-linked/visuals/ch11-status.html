11.0

Status and purpose of the Conceptual Framework

Opens the Framework, ahead of its eight chapters · not a Standard and overrides no Standard or requirement · three purposes · foundation for Standards.

Purpose

11.1Three main uses

Board

  • Develop Standards on consistent concepts
  • A few decisions only the Board can take (when to use OCI)

Preparers

  • Consistent policies when no Standard applies
  • Or when a Standard allows a choice

All parties

  • Understand and interpret the Standards
  • Official Three uses side by side; a Board-only purpose rejected
  • DTT Main purpose is to guide the IASB
  • EY Summary leads with the Board; detail lists all three
  • KPMG Primarily a tool for the IASB and the Interpretations Committee
  • Sources differ · not reconciled

Around the Standards

11.4Departures and revisions

  • Depart only to meet the objective; explained in the Basis for Conclusions
  • Revising the Framework does not amend Standards — due process
  • Interpretations Committee refers inconsistencies to the Board
  • Official Departures can be appropriate
  • EY Standards that contravene the Framework
  • Sources differ · not reconciled

11.5Foundation role

  • Transparency — comparable, higher-quality information
  • Accountability — information to hold management to account
  • Economic efficiency — better capital allocation

11.6References and effective date

  • Board and Committee use the 2018 Framework at once
  • EY No explicit date; broadly January 2020
  • DTT From 1 January 2020; earlier application permitted
  • KPMG Effective immediately; policies retrospective from 1 January 2020
  • Sources differ · not reconciled
  • Unamended Standard wording prevails
  • DTT 2001 and 2010 versions
  • EY 1989 and 2010 Frameworks
  • Sources differ · not reconciled
